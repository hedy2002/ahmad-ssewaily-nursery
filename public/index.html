Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers for new plant parents
How do you deliver plants?

We confirm your location by phone and arrange careful local delivery with the order details.

How often should I water my plant?

Check the care badges on each product. Most indoor plants prefer weekly watering, while low-water plants need much less.

Can I order directly through WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp button on any product card or product page to contact the nursery directly.

What should I do if a product is out of stock?

Open its product page and submit your email or phone number. We will keep your request in the availability list.
